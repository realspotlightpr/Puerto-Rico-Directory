# SEO operating plan — August 29, 2026

## Decision

Stop treating 500 generated URLs as the success metric. The database currently contains 40 approved businesses across only two municipalities, with 39 in Arecibo. Search visibility should expand from useful first-party inventory, not municipality-name substitution.

## Release gates

A municipality/category page is indexable only when it has:

1. At least two approved matching businesses.
2. Unique visible listing content, not only generic prose.
3. A self-referencing canonical and inclusion in the programmatic sitemap.
4. Links to usable business profiles and the filtered directory.
5. Accurate claims based on stored business data.

The minimum of two is an initial technical floor, not a guarantee of quality. Raise it as inventory grows. Editorial travel pages require separate human-quality research and should not be generated from business counts.

## Current first-party inventory

- 40 approved businesses.
- 39 in Arecibo and one in Guaynabo.
- 31 have descriptions, 29 addresses, 27 phone numbers, and 19 websites.
- Nine named categories; ten records currently have no category.

## Next work queue

1. Deploy the inventory-gated generator and unique business SEO shells.
2. Verify sitemap files return XML rather than the SPA homepage.
3. Verify sample business and Arecibo category pages expose unique source metadata.
4. Submit the sitemap index in Search Console.
5. Improve the ten uncategorized listings and missing contact fields.
6. Publish original English and Spanish Arecibo editorial hubs based on local sources and first-hand/merchant input.
7. Acquire additional approved businesses municipality by municipality.
8. Expand indexable pages only after inventory and Search Console evidence support them.

## Measurement

Track generated, deployed, submitted, discovered, indexed, receiving impressions, receiving clicks, and converting as separate states. Never report a generated URL as published or ranked.

Search volume and difficulty are intentionally not fabricated in the keyword map. Add those fields after Search Console access or a trusted keyword-data export becomes available.
