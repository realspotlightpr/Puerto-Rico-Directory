# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1
**Last updated:** 2026-08-29

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Spotlight Puerto Rico helps residents and visitors discover local businesses, places, and experiences across Puerto Rico.
**What it does:** The platform combines a searchable local-business directory with activities, experiences, travel planning, and business promotion. Businesses can submit or claim listings, while consumers can browse trustworthy local options.
**Product category:** Puerto Rico local business directory and discovery platform.
**Product type:** Two-sided local marketplace and directory.
**Business model:** Free consumer discovery with paid promotion, advertising, enhanced listings, and related business services.

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Puerto Rico restaurants, retailers, service providers, attractions, guides, and experience operators.
**Decision-makers:** Owners, operators, and marketing managers of local businesses.
**Primary use case:** Help consumers find credible local options and help Puerto Rico businesses earn discovery, traffic, and inquiries.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Find places to eat, shop, visit, and hire in a specific Puerto Rico municipality.
- Compare current business information before visiting or contacting a provider.
- Give local businesses a discoverable profile they can claim and improve.
**Use cases:** Local dining, travel planning, activity discovery, service-provider research, business promotion, and itinerary building.

| Resident or visitor | Relevant nearby choices and trustworthy details | Information is fragmented or outdated | One local discovery destination organized by municipality and category |
| Local business owner | Visibility, inquiries, and reputation | Competing with chains and larger platforms | A focused Puerto Rico profile and promotion channel |

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Puerto Rico discovery information is scattered across maps, social accounts, tourism sites, and outdated directories.
**Why alternatives fall short:** Broad global platforms lack local focus; social profiles are difficult to compare; many directories contain incomplete or stale records.
**What it costs them:** Consumers lose planning time and businesses miss visits, inquiries, and bookings.
**Emotional tension:** Visitors worry about wasting limited vacation time; owners worry that strong local businesses remain invisible.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Discover Puerto Rico partner directory and local directory sites — often tourism-led, eligibility-limited, or less focused on small-business self-service.
**Secondary:** Google Maps, Yelp, Tripadvisor, and social platforms — broad reach but limited Puerto Rico-specific editorial structure.
**Indirect:** Word of mouth and unstructured social recommendations — authentic but inconsistent and difficult to search.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Puerto Rico-only focus spanning all 78 municipalities.
- Business profiles connected to local activities, experiences, and planning tools.
- First-party merchant submissions, claims, reviews, and updates.
**How we do it differently:** Organize discovery around real local inventory rather than generic city-keyword pages.
**Why that's better:** Searchers reach usable business information quickly and businesses gain contextually relevant visibility.
**Why customers choose us:** Local relevance, breadth of discovery, and a clear path for businesses to participate.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| “The listing information may be outdated.” | Show update dates, verified fields, and a simple claim/correction workflow. |
| “Google Maps already does this.” | Provide Puerto Rico-specific curation, editorial context, and cross-links between businesses and experiences. |
| “There are not enough listings in my town yet.” | Index only useful inventory-backed pages and expand as local coverage grows. |

**Anti-persona:** Businesses seeking fabricated reviews, misleading claims, spam links, or guaranteed Google rankings.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Fragmented discovery and weak visibility for independent businesses.
**Pull:** A dedicated Puerto Rico audience and structured local profiles.
**Habit:** Consumers default to global maps and social feeds.
**Anxiety:** Concern about data accuracy and whether a newer directory has enough coverage.
